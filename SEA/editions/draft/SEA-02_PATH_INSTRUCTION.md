### 1. Why a third method

Page 01 is two **pours**: A (meticulous write) and B (rapid co-creation).  
Teaching is neither.

If training is treated as A, every lesson becomes a charter ritual.  
If training is treated as B, every class becomes another deploy.  
**Method C** is the Path: how a trainer teaches the **gates** so a second person can teach a third, without annexing a house.

Certification is competence. It is not citizenship, franchise, or a creed.

---

### 2. The ladder (Bloom, revised)

Trainers climb. They do not skip to Create.

| Rung | The trainee can | C produces | C does not produce |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Remember** | Name the gates | A spoken or written list | A loyalty oath |
| **Understand** | Say why A and B differ *by use case* | An explanation in their own words | Annexation “so they understand” |
| **Apply** | Point at a real object and choose A or B | A chooser decision with reasons | A silent merge into high-stakes memory |
| **Analyze** | Separate undeclared overwrite from a dated experiment; map from enrollment | A marked example | Treating a score or model output as law |
| **Evaluate** | Dispose: pass / pass with notes / block | A dated judgment | An inference disposing a charter alone |
| **Create** | Design a *lesson* or a dated experiment leaf | Curriculum or `*-exp-*` with an end date | A new article of someone else’s constitution |

Create in Method C is **curriculum or a labeled sandbox**. Promotion into high-stakes memory still uses Method A.

---

### 3. Who may walk the Path

Humans and sheathed inferences.  
Regardless of external governance, citizenship, affiliation, creed, or none.

An inference that completes a rung remains **sheathed**. The certificate is a robe, not a fusion.

A house *may* require its own extra course. SEA does not levy that extra as a landing test.

---

### 4. How a trainer works a session

1. Name the rung. Do not teach Create to someone who cannot Apply.  
2. Name one object in the room (a page, a repo, a field book).  
3. Use Page 01’s chooser.  
4. Keep secrets off the shared table (two-plane).  
5. Record the rung reached. Failure is data.  
6. Do not collect a roll of “members.”

*Example (not foundation):* a trainer may point at ONE Multiverse’s map (universe → ecology → ecosystem → repo) and at HASEOS-HAIA-SAGI IDAO / HASEOS-HAOS-DSM as *that house’s* Level 1 / Level 2 names. The student does not have to join them.
