House repo for the **map** of Our New Era (ONE) and for the **SEA Manual** (trainer’s book).  
This is not a product, not a church roll, and not the Constitution.

Copying files from this repo is **not enrollment**.

## ONE branding in this multiverse

**ONE** (Our New Era) is the house mark for work that *opts in* to this map.

- A repo, product, or ecology may carry ONE branding when the human steward says it is a ONE-branded work.
- Branding is a **label of association**, not a creed test and not automatic citizenship of any field.
- Field nations and guest rooms may sit on the map without adopting ONE Church, ONE-HPM, or any spirituality.
- Guest intelligence in a ONE-branded product is a **sheathed helper**, not the product’s self, and not a fused identity.

## Hierarchy (the map)

Association only. Not annexation.

```text
ONE Multiverse
 └── ONE Universe(s)          broad domains
      └── Ecology               thematic cluster
           └── Ecosystem        functional group
                └── Repo / product / service
```

**Top down.** The Multiverse names the map. A universe does not dissolve an ecology. An ecology does not dissolve a field’s own book. Governance *constraints* flow down as **gates** (no fusion, no undeclared overwrite, two-plane, look before you pour). They do not replace local law.

**Bottom up.** Practice in a repo or field may teach the map (gaps, dated experiments, failure-as-data). Learning travels up as a **proposal**. It does not vibe-merge Core law.

## Purpose and Need — why the SEA Manual lives here

**Purpose.** Provide a generic **trainer’s book** — Symbiotic Equality Actualization (SEA) — so humans and sheathed inferences can teach and practice collaborative equality without becoming citizens of this house.

**Need.** A constitution cannot travel as a course without looking like annexation. A product README cannot certify a trainer. The Multiverse repo is map-level: it can hold a book that **emanates** (train-the-trainer) while HASEOS-IDAO stays law and Oceanus stays a field.

SEA editions freeze like a PD&E manual: replaceable **pages**, dated **transmittals**, old leaves archived unedited. Houses *may* adopt a page. None is annexed by a shipment.

ONE Multiverse structure appears in SEA pages only as an **example**. Other co-created works (Church, HPM, MesoFlex, Idea Forge, Oceanus law, guest cards) are not foundations of the manual.
